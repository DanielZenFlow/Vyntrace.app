Your AI usage, kept quietly.

Vyntrace saves the numbers from Claude Reflect and ChatGPT Analytics in your own browser, then brings the month to your iPhone: one plant, one big figure, a few small pictures.

Get the Chrome extension iPhone appcoming soon

Three steps, no account

There is nothing to sign up for. You sign in with your own Google or iCloud, and your data only travels between your own devices and your own drive.

01

Save

The extension keeps the reports exactly as they are in your browser when you open Claude Reflect or ChatGPT Analytics. It does not grow without end: only the newest copies of each month are kept.

02

Send

Press “Sign in with Google” once, or choose a folder in iCloud Drive. It only writes to its own folder, “Vyntrace Sync”.

03

See

The iPhone app and widgets read that folder and draw the month. Once the phone has read it, the extension shows “On your iPhone”.

Your data stays yours

We run no server and cannot see any of your data.
